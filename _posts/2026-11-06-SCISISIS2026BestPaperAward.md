国際会議SCIS&ISIS2026において，下記の共著論文がBest Paper Awardを受賞しました．

The following co-authored paper received the Best Paper Award at SCIS&ISIS2026.

=出典情報=

Y. Kato, Y. Toda, N. Masuyama, Y. Wang, and T. Matsuno, "Two-stage topological clustering with self-adjusting vigilance for adaptive perception," in Proc. of the 2026 Joint 14th International Conference on Soft Computing and Intelligent Systems and 27th International Symposium on Advanced Intelligent Systems (SCIS&ISIS2026), pp. 1-6, Hachioji, Japan, November 2-5, 2026.
